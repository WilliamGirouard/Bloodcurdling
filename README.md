# Bloodcurdling

A tense, low-poly 3D survival horror game built in Unity. Navigate a pitch-black forest, evade a relentless monster, and scour the shadows for the objects you need to unlock your escape.

---

## About the Game

**Bloodcurdling** drops you deep inside an oppressive, atmospheric woodland where every sound could give away your position. Hunted by a dynamic monster, your only chance of survival is scavenging key objects hidden across the forest to open the sealed door or trap standing between you and escape.

---

## Core Features

* **Atmospheric Environment:** Low-poly forest aesthetics paired with dark, moody lighting and dense foliage.
* **Relentless Monster AI:** A dangerous creature patrolling the woods that hunts you down as you search.
* **Object Retrieval Gameplay:** Explore the dark forest to locate specific items required to trigger the exit door or trap mechanism.
* **Tense Survival:** Manage your movement, stay out of sight, and time your actions carefully to make it out alive.

---

## Controls

* **WASD / Arrow Keys:** Move
* **Shift:** Sprint
* **E:** Interact / Pick up objects
* **C / Ctrl:** Crouch

---

## Built With

* **Engine:** Unity (3D Pipeline)
* **Scripting:** C#
* **Assets:** Low-poly 3D environmental models, custom scripts, and atmospheric audio.
